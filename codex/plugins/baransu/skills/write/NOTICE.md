What was adapted, in this skill's own words: the "say what it does, not how it feels" test in SKILL.md's Direct language section; the shared principles S1–S5 in `references/writing-principles.md` (chatbot framing, vague attribution, hedge stacking, format tells, over-compression), each given a Traditional Chinese counterpart; the en principles 7–10 (AI lexicon, hollow tails and signposts, rule of three, format tells) and the metaphor-noun note under en principle 1. Not adopted: unslop's ban on parentheses and hyphens as dash substitutes (en rule 8 already sets the dash policy) and its numbered rule ids (`$baransu:write` tags rules by name).
