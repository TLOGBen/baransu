# Third-party notice — /baransu:think

The Reframe and Sharpen sections of `/think` are adapted from the `reframe` skill (`pstack/skills/reframe/`, as of commit `9e278fe`, 2026-11-24) and the `sharpen` skill (`pstack/skills/sharpen/`, as of commit `bf0c7cd`, 2026-11-15) in Cursor's pstack plugin ([cursor/plugins](https://github.com/cursor/plugins)), by Lauren Tan, under the MIT License reproduced below.

What was kept, in this skill's own words: from `reframe`, listing the candidate problems a vague description could be with the term and the telling sign for each, 8 to 12 terms ranked by how much they change what gets built with a confirmed link each, 2 or 3 neighbouring fields with where the analogy breaks, what the user did not mention, and the rewrite in the new words; from `sharpen`, the four questions (the professional's move, the rawest material, the methods and tools, the outside judge), the rewrite that is deeper but not bigger, and prototyping any choice an experiment can settle while leaving product and preference choices to the user. What changed: both run inside `/think` as its opening, before the restatement; reframe loops with the user until the rewrite is accepted; sharpen absorbs `/think`'s former borrow step and its quantitative model; an experiment that needs code becomes the plan's pilot check, since `/think` writes no code.
