## Sources by reference file

The motion references carry no source lines of their own; their provenance is recorded here, file by file, so the files an agent reads stay instructions only.

- `references/motion/2-draft.md` — the reads, events, stage / life / focus and rhythm rules are distilled from `JohnHeibel/ClaudeAnimationBase` ANIMATION_GUIDE (MIT) and `buildwithhanif/claude-animation-skill` references (MIT); the full originals are in `hand-drawn/`. The frame-range example follows a public X article by @0xCodila (2026-10-05). The direction funnel (wide → 3 → 1), the cinematography plan, the colour script, the pacing curve and the timing sheet are this skill's own, built on standard animation and film practice.
- `references/motion/beat-grid.md` — the sync-to-frame + 1 rule, the measurement table and the scoring-from-frames examples are restated from a public X article by @flxrnc (2026-09-30); the sound rules from real films and the beds are this skill's own (the beds are in the vendored hand-drawn library).
- `references/motion/camera.md` — the 24 names, their grouping and their HUD readouts follow a public X post by @showheyohtaki (2026-10-11), "動画のカメラワークと切り替えの名前", restated in this skill's own words; the director-note examples follow a public X post by @rexan_wong (2026-09-26). The drawing recipes are this skill's own.
- `references/motion/chapters.md` — the chapter/guide/subagent structure is described from `JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo` (ISC); the chunked renderer, picture-derived sound and narration timing from the public description of `WinterArc21/Battle-of-Austerlitz-Film` (no license granted, so nothing from it is copied — only the pattern is described).
- `references/motion/commission.md` — the workflow, the example storyboard, the spring baseline, the review and packaging rules, the pricing method, the proposal shape and the market snapshot are restated from a public X article by @0xCodila (2026-10-05, a paid partnership with an AI research assistant whose tool-specific steps are generalised here); the reference-and-components advice follows a public X post by @rexan_wong (2026-09-26).
- `references/motion/composition.md` — the 6 : 3 : 1 rule, its dimensions table, the colour, lighting and detail applications, the film reading (space → life → plot focus, with The Grand Budapest Hotel, Dune, Parasite, Inception, Schindler's List), the five starting questions, the thumbnail test, the scene prompt structure and the video notes are restated from a public X article by @nanyuan0412 (2026-10-01), crediting 耿嘉辉《游戏美术中的 631 法则》; the mapping to stage / life / focus, key cards and the critique row is this skill's own.
- `references/motion/finishing.md` — restated from a public X article by @flxrnc (2026-09-30), citing X's media documentation and Help Center; the mapping to this skill's `render.mjs`, `sfx.mjs` and `checks.sh` is this skill's own.
- `references/mechanism/mechanism-catalog.md` — concepts, objects, interactions, numbers and prompts restated from the Anatomy collection by Ryan (@wheresryan22), skills.wheresryan.sh/anatomy/collection and github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy (MIT), read 2026-10-12.
- `references/mechanism/mechanism.md` — the method, the sizes, the drawing, motion, shader and verification rules, the timings and the examples are restated from Anatomy by Ryan (@wheresryan22): skills.wheresryan.sh/anatomy, its "How I built it" page, and github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy (SKILL.md and references/craft.md, motion.md, verify.md, walkthrough.md; MIT), read 2026-10-12. Its drawing kit and scripts are not bundled; the mechanism-family table and the mapping template in § Inventing your own are this skill's own synthesis of the published examples.
- `references/motion/pipeline.md` — the render contract lines on pinned libraries, network, CSS / Web Animations and loops, the engines table, the minimal renderer and the runtime setup are restated from a public X article by @flxrnc (2026-09-30), citing the engines' own documentation and licences; the rest is this skill's own.
- `references/motion/prompts.md` — the ladder, the showreel analysis and the brief skeleton follow a public X article by @0xMovez (2026-09-27) and the public posts it cites, restated in this skill's own words; the one-line showreel prompt and the published XML tag names are quoted as they circulated. Dataset figures come from `athemeroy/awesome-opus-5-5-videos` (CC BY 4.0) and `guanmo-ai/awesome-ai-motion` (MIT). The directed-prompt template, the A/B evidence, the chrome-loop prompt and the receipts are restated from a public X article by @flxrnc (2026-09-30).
- `references/motion/review.md` — the frame-by-URL hook, the animator views, the follow-up prompts, the feelings table, the frame-pair diff, the numeric audit and the fixes table are restated from a public X article by @flxrnc, "Opus 5.5 Motion Lab: Make Claude Animate Like a Studio" (2026-09-30); the export review from a public X article by @0xCodila (2026-10-05); the first-time-viewer reading is distilled from `JohnHeibel/ClaudeAnimationBase` ANIMATION_GUIDE (MIT) and `buildwithhanif/claude-animation-skill` references (MIT).
- `references/motion/springs.md` — the easing table, the damping-ratio translation, the durations and stagger, the hierarchy rule and the principles-as-numbers table are restated from a public X article by @flxrnc (2026-09-30), which cites the Easings reference (Andrey Sitnik), Material 3 motion, Nielsen Norman Group and Thomas & Johnston's *The Illusion of Life*; the gather baseline from a public X article by @0xCodila (2026-10-05). The feel ratios and the k / d conversion are this skill's own.

Everything under `scripts/motion/`, `assets/motion/`, `references/motion/*.md` (other than `hand-drawn/` and the attributed paraphrases above), `references/critique-loop.md`, `references/loop-pauses.md` and `SKILL.md` is original to this plugin and carries its license.

Not bundled on purpose: Remotion and HyperFrames agent skills (their workflows fetch updates over the network and one has no license file), the ClaudeAnimationBase character kit (p5.brush + puppeteer runtime), and the song, lyrics and media assets of any production above.
