# Third-party notice — /baransu:teach-me

`/teach-me` is adapted from the `teach` skill in Cursor's pstack plugin ([cursor/plugins](https://github.com/cursor/plugins), `pstack/skills/teach/`, retrieved 2026-10-17), by Lauren Tan, under the MIT License reproduced below. The name changed from `teach` to `teach-me`.

What was kept, in this skill's own words: choosing the few things to walk away with from the conversation, building on `how` and `why` instead of redoing their work, keeping `why`'s confidence language intact, starting from a plain definition, the smallest complete answer first, conversation over performance, and diagrams built up one part at a time. What changed: a `/learn` brief or digest is a second kind of material for topics outside the repo; the writing style points to `/write`'s rules instead of the `unslop` skill, and the style items `/write` already covers are not repeated; spatial figures go to `/draw` instead of an image-generation tool. No upstream file is copied.
